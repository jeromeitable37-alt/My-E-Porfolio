MY REFLECTION

What I learned about myself.

MY THOUGHTS Pause.
Think.
Respond.

I sometimes take things personally, especially when it comes to criticism, failed tests, or conflict with friends. When I am angry, disappointed, or stressed, I can make decisions too quickly.

One thing I want to improve is how I manage my emotions. I am learning to pause first, think about the situation, and then respond instead of reacting right away.

I also want to manage my time better. When I plan my schoolwork earlier, I feel less stressed and I can focus better.

MY GOAL To make better decisions, communicate better, and handle difficult situations more calmly.

RANDOM ACTS OF KINDNESS

Six weeks, small actions.

I learned that kindness can be simple. Most of the things I wrote down were ordinary actions, but they still matter.

WEEK 1

Greet office staff politely, thank someone sincerely, help organize documents, speak respectfully, and pick up trash even when it is not mine.

WEEK 2

Help a nursing student who lost his contribution money, greet office employees while routing documents, and help arrange documents in the purchasing office.

WEEK 3

Clean my assigned duties properly, say thank you and good morning, and hold a door open for someone.

WEEK 4

Greet employees when giving a document, clean my dorm duties properly, and ask someone if they need help when they look overwhelmed.

WEEK 5

Offer help to classmates who are having difficulty, greet school staff politely, and keep my surroundings clean.

WEEK 6

Speak politely when asking for help or directions, return borrowed items properly, say thank you, and stay calm when dealing with others.
